## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vercel deployment, and Resend for transactional lead notifications. This supports a fast marketing site with a secure, maintainable contact flow.

## Users

Primary users are Fresno-area property managers, facilities managers, and business owners who need a dependable local pest-control partner for an active or recurring pest issue. Secondary users are local homeowners seeking an estimate or service.

## Product Purpose

The Bug Dude Pest Control helps Central Valley customers solve pest problems with approachable, responsive service. The website should turn Google search traffic into calls and qualified commercial-service leads, while making online estimate requests easy to manage.

## Positioning

Confirmed current-site language positions the business as a local, family-friend-style provider with same-day availability when possible, satisfaction follow-up at no added charge, and flexible one-time or no-contract options. Commercial-specific proof and service terms remain to be confirmed.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive from local search on mobile and need a fast answer to: can this company handle my pest issue, service my property, and be reached today? Leads should flow by click-to-call, online estimate request, and an optional external booking link managed by the site owner.

## Product Principles

1. Put the immediate next step—call or request an estimate—within reach from every key page.
2. Make commercial visitors feel specifically served without overstating unverified experience.
3. Make lead handling simple: one concise form, clear email delivery, and configurable booking handoff.
4. Translate local search intent into helpful, specific service information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, accessible forms and error messages, keyboard support, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
